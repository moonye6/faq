## Strategic implications

1. **Entry IS possible.** Multiple 2024-2026 indie entrants (SeoBotAi, iLoveSchema, Byword, LocalHQ, Recipe Kit, jsonld.com v2) rank in top 10 for primary keywords. The market isn't owned by giants alone.

2. **Vertical specialization beats generic.** Recipe Kit (vertical: recipe sites only) outranks generic tools for "recipe schema generator." Same pattern likely holds for other verticals (HowTo for tutorials, Product for e-commerce, JobPosting for HR-tech).

3. **GEO/AEO angle is genuinely differentiated.** None of the top 50 tools surveyed lead with "your schema feeds AI search citations" framing. Most still pitch SERP rich snippets (which we know is dying for FAQ/HowTo). **The faqjsonld.com angle is real and unoccupied — at least for now.**

4. **Long-tail keywords are wide open.** Specific keywords like "course schema generator," "breadcrumb schema generator," "organization schema generator" are likely much less competitive (TBD pending paid-tool data).
